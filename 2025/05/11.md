## 🧠 English

### ✅ What I did
- [ ] Deepened understanding of nouns, pronouns, and articles

### 💡 What I noticed / learned
- Use “the” with things that share common knowledge  
- “the + plural” refers to a specific group (very useful!)  
- No-article plurals express general categories  
- Uncountable nouns lack a clear shape/form  
- “you” can be generic/general  
- “we” includes the speaker (e.g., “We had a lot of rain in Tokyo yesterday.”)  
- Use “they” for regions/persons when speaker is excluded (e.g., “They had a lot of rain in Okinawa yesterday.”)  
- “they” contrasts with “we,” and can refer to “people in general”  
- “almost” is an adverb and does not modify nouns  
- After “of” in “part of,” the noun must be specific—defining the whole to talk about a part

- Continue memorizing the grammar reference book  
- Use template repetition to actively apply the grammar I’ve learned
